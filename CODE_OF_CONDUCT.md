# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people, regardless of their identity or background. We pledge that everyone participating in the AQI Predictor community will experience a harassment-free environment.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Assuming good intent in conversations
- Being patient with those new to the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Unwelcome sexual advances or attention
- Publishing private information without consent
- Deliberate intimidation or stalking
- Disruptive behavior in conversations
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Commit messages and comments
- Code review feedback
- Email and direct messages
- Any representation of the project in public spaces

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation of this Code of Conduct, please report it by emailing:

**ayyan.storm@github.com**

Include:
- Description of the behavior
- Context and when it occurred
- Who was involved
- Any relevant links or screenshots

Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.

### Response

Community leaders will:

1. **Acknowledge** receipt within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** the report fairly and thoroughly
3. **Respond** with appropriate action
4. **Follow up** with the reporter when safe to do so

### Consequences

Violations may result in:

- Warning or request to modify behavior
- Temporary mute or suspension from discussions
- Temporary or permanent ban from the community
- Report to GitHub Trust & Safety team

The severity of consequences depends on:
- Nature of the violation
- Severity and impact
- Repeat offenses
- Malicious intent

## Examples of How to Handle Conflict

### If someone is being disruptive:
- **Don't:** Engage in escalation
- **Do:** Respectfully ask them to stop
- **If continues:** Report to maintainers

### If you disagree with someone:
- **Don't:** Use personal attacks or insults
- **Do:** Focus on the idea, not the person
- **Example:** "I think X approach has drawbacks because..." instead of "Your idea is stupid"

### If you made a mistake:
- **Don't:** Deny or deflect blame
- **Do:** Apologize sincerely and explain how you'll do better
- **Example:** "I apologize for that comment. It was disrespectful. I'll be more mindful in the future."

## Leadership Responsibilities

Project maintainers will:

- Model respectful behavior
- Address violations promptly and fairly
- Explain decisions transparently
- Recuse themselves from conflicts of interest
- Maintain confidentiality of reporters
- Welcome feedback on their own conduct

## Questions or Feedback

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct or want to provide feedback:

- Open a discussion in GitHub Discussions
- Email ayyan.storm@github.com
- Comment on issues related to community guidelines
